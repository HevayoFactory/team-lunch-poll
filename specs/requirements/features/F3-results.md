# Results

## Purpose

Shows everyone the winning lunch option once voting closes at 11am.

Needs: F2.

## User Stories

- F3.1 As a teammate, I see the winning option for today's poll once voting
closes at 11am, whether or not I voted.
- F3.2 As a teammate, I look back at past polls and see what won each day.

## Decisions

- When votes tie for first place, one of the tied options is picked at random
as the winner.
- If no votes are cast before 11am, the poll closes with no winner shown.
- Past polls and their results stay visible indefinitely.
