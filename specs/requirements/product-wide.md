# Product-wide

Rules that apply to more than one feature.

## Requirements

- P1 Voting on a poll closes at 11am on the poll's day; no votes are accepted
after that. Applies to: F2, F3. *assumed*
- P2 Every teammate signs in via single sign-on before proposing a poll or
voting. \[org default\] Applies to: all.
